THE LOOT PHILOSOPHY

The Art of Haute Parfumerie

"Luxury is never about ostentation; luxury is the invisible aura of presence you leave behind."

LOOT PERFUMES was founded by Nasif Ali with an uncompromising mandate: to bridge the timeless opulence of Middle Eastern perfumery with the delicate balance of French haute formulation.

While mass-market fragrances dilute their oils to 10%–15%, every creation in the LOOT collection is compounded as an Extrait de Parfum with 32%–38% pure perfume oils. The result is an extraordinary 18-hour sillage that evolves beautifully with your skin chemistry throughout the day and into the night.
